# Board brief: MyLoveLab

This brief stands on its own. The founders cannot be asked questions during the meeting. Facts below come from the founders' own context document (dated 19 Nov 2026) and the public site. Anything not stated here is unknown; do not invent it.

## The business in one sentence
MyLoveLab (mylovelab.fr) is a French-language, web-only B2C subscription for couples: a free relationship test as the front door, then a daily programme of short playful sessions (~8 min) so partners understand each other better and the relationship lasts. Public promise: "Mieux vous comprendre à deux, en quelques minutes par jour" (understand each other better, as a couple, in a few minutes a day).

## The product
- 7 dimensions: communication and listening; attachment and security; love languages; conflict handling; intimacy and desire; trust and support; shared project.
- 10 levels × 6 sessions per dimension = 420 sessions of ~8 minutes. Content for all 7 dimensions is written (8,000–10,000 words each); not every module is online yet.
- 24 relationship profiles (4 attachment styles × 6 relational patterns). Score out of 100 per dimension; daily sessions target the weakest dimension first.
- Couple mode: mirrored profiles, one shared subscription for two.
- Gamification: streaks, levels, Duolingo-style feedback sounds planned.
- Scientific grounding claimed: Gottman, Sue Johnson, Esther Perel, Bowlby/Ainsworth, Chapman, Schnarch, Nagoski, Fraley, Bodenmann (dyadic stress; the public test is framed around how outside stress damages couple communication).
- Editorial rules: no fixed labels ("you are like this"), speak of "playful sessions", avoid words like diagnosis, therapy, "save your relationship". It does not replace a therapist; couples in acute crisis are referred to a practitioner; domestic violence → the 3919 hotline.
- Web app (no native mobile app). Stack: Vercel, Supabase, Stripe (with affiliate promo codes and ?ref= links), Microsoft Clarity, GA4; Meta and TikTok pixels to set up before ads.

## Price
- Free entry test (a 0.99 € option is being considered).
- "Essentiel": ~9.90 €/month. "Premium Duo": ~19.90 €/month (shared by both partners).
- To test: an annual plan for two at 39–49 €, invited partner free. Gift card "Duo 3 months" at 49 € planned.
- Founders want a ceiling around 10 €/month to stay accessible.
- Reference competitor named by the founders: Paired (mobile app, 14.99 €/month in France per their note; the French App Store rating is ~4.6/5 on ~3,100 ratings; reviews complain about price and a thin free tier).

## Customer
- Launch target: women 25–35, in a relationship for 2–7 years, living with their partner (from moving in together to the first child), in France.
- Then: young parents; then men 30–45 via SEO.
- Not now: couples in acute crisis; over-50s.
- Practitioners (psychologists, couple therapists, sexologists): a credibility and referral channel, not a volume channel. They get 30% affiliate commission for 12 months and a free Duo, with cross-links.
- Market notes from the founders: ~15.5M couples in France; core 25–39 estimated at 3.5–4M; 34% of separated 30–49-year-olds consider their breakup avoidable, first lever cited is "communicate better" (35%). Their own finding: the need is real but spontaneous demand is low; people come out of curiosity (the test) or in a moment of tension.

## Traction so far
- Site live since summer 2026 (~5 months), open to the public, partially indexed, with a blog and an English section.
- Mid-September: fewer than 20 people had taken the test. Current figure unknown.
- 160 practitioners contacted in two waves; a handful of positive replies (4 named).
- Cold channels (Instagram DMs, cold emails, Facebook groups) worked poorly for such an intimate product.
- Instagram: 2 posts a week.
- Brand: heart-with-arrow logo, main colour #FC355A. Former name MyRelation (domain migration from app.myrelation.fr planned).
